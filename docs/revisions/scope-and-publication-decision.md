# Scope and Publication Decision

**Project:** *From Question to Evidence: A Living Field Guide for Qualitative Research*  
**Decision date:** 25 July 2026  
**Status:** Working route approved by Ami Pedahzur; Jonathan Grossman's confirmation remains required before publication of the expanded draft.

## Decision

The project will proceed as a long-form, open-access living field guide. It will not be reduced to a short guide or Element concerned only with building an evidence base.

The book will follow a qualitative project from its first question to a documented corpus, an analysis, and a public claim. The first edition will connect evidence mapping, literature, collection, preparation, analysis, claim testing, writing, responsible sharing, and maintenance. English remains the canonical first edition. A Hebrew adaptation will follow after the content model and navigation stabilize.

## Publication Model

The field guide will be published in reviewed modules. Incremental publication does not divide the project into unrelated products. Each module belongs to one book, uses the established page pattern, and produces artifacts that preserve consequential research decisions.

The current `0.2.0-draft` contains the first two complete modules: From Question to Evidence Map and Literature as Evidence. The `0.3.0-draft` revision program will add an early chapter on epistemic judgment and limits, move AI integrity before the first guided workflow, carry a composite oral-history study through the method, provide complete artifact specimens, and add Collection as Evidence.

Later modules will address preparation, analysis, disconfirmation, writing, sharing, and maintenance. Version `1.0.0` will require a documented reader pilot, stable templates, verified citations, cross-format inspection, and approval from both authors.

## Organizing Architecture

The guide treats the research record as a scholarly Second Brain. Capture, organization, distillation, and expression remain connected to evidence, provenance, ethical limits, and scholarly judgment. The system is not a warehouse. It is a reviewable chain linking a question to a corpus, a corpus to an analysis, and an analysis to a claim.

Generative AI remains a bounded assistant. It may help expand vocabulary, generate alternatives, compare patterns, and challenge a search strategy. It may not establish that a source exists, that a claim is true, or that collection is complete. Privacy, verification, accountability, and responsibility remain with the researcher.

## Required Commitments

- Retain the title *From Question to Evidence* and the subtitle *A Living Field Guide for Qualitative Research*.
- Add Collection as Evidence rather than treating collection as deferred future work.
- Keep the documentary terrorism example and add a complete composite oral-history example using no real participant data.
- State the guide's epistemological commitments and limits before the first procedural stage.
- Separate durable method from product-specific AI instructions.
- Publish stable methodological pages alongside editable resources and dated tool guidance.
- Treat English as canonical during development and Hebrew as a later adaptation.
- Do not issue the expanded public release until both authors approve its scope and rendered manuscript.

## Consequence for the Current Draft

The opening, contents, README, roadmap, and repository surfaces must describe the same destination while distinguishing completed modules from planned ones. The current two modules are the tested beginning of the full book. They are not the book's final scope.
