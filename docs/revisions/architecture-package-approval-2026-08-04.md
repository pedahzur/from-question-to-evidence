## Approved package

1. **Six-part architecture:**
   - Part I — Judgment, Evidence, and AI
   - Part II — Designing the Evidence Base
   - Part III — Literature as Evidence
   - Part IV — Collection and Preparation
   - Part V — Analysis and Claim Testing
   - Part VI — Writing, Sharing, and Maintaining
2. **Chapter-disposition map:** the current twenty-seven rendered chapters will be retained, split, relocated, companionized, replaced, or consolidated according to the disposition map in the gap-closure plan. The target is approximately 32–36 numbered chapters rather than forty-five short content units.
3. **Ten-stage master lifecycle:** Position; Frame; Design evidence; Discover; Collect; Prepare; Analyze; Test claims; Express; Release and maintain. Domain-specific sequences become views over this lifecycle.
4. **Composite municipal oral-history case:** the participant-produced evidence pathway will use an invented/composited study of tenants' experiences and memories of municipal housing redevelopment between the 1970s and 1990s. No real participant data will be used.
5. **Provisional length and companion boundary:** the target body length is 75,000–90,000 words, excluding references and companion documentation. Stable method belongs in the numbered book; volatile tools, installation guidance, benchmarks, and extended implementation materials belong in companion resources.
