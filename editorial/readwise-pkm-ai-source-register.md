# Readwise PKM and AI Source Register

## Audit Scope

This register documents the read-only Reader audit used to draft
`content/20-pkm-and-ai-research-infrastructure.qmd`.

Reader reported the following tag-level counts on July 27, 2026:

| Reader tag | Reported records | Detailed metadata retrieved |
|---|---:|---:|
| `📄Academic Article` | 567 | 500 |
| `🗃️PKM` | 499 | 499 |
| `🤖AI` | 3,196 | 500 |

The Reader connector limit returned the most recently added 500 academic and AI
records even though the tag index reported larger totals. The PKM set was retrieved
in full. The audit therefore uses the PKM chronology as the longitudinal backbone
and uses the academic and AI records as bounded samples. It does not claim a
complete chronology of either larger tag.

Tags are organizational labels, not assessments of source quality. The `🤖AI` set
includes scholarly work, journalism, product announcements, social-media threads,
duplicates, and records outside research methods. The chapter does not treat the
tagged collection as a formal literature corpus.

## Integrity Decisions

The chapter does not quote social-media promotional language as established fact.
It uses those records as evidence of changing PKM practice and of the user’s
attention over time.

The chapter identifies the “self-evolving AI scientist” claim as provisional and
requires verification against the cited arXiv paper. It identifies the “Karpaty’s
Second Brain” item as a derivative conversation rather than the original video.
It retains the `redundant` status attached to the shorter Obsidian item.

No Reader documents, tags, locations, notes, or highlights were modified.
