# Voice Workflow Chapter Update

Date: 2026-08-01

## Decision

The English-first manuscript now includes **Writing by Voice, Revising by Ear**
after the PKM and AI research-infrastructure chapter. The chapter treats AI
dictation and text-to-speech as two stages in one inspectable writing process,
not as product features or substitutes for scholarly judgment.

## Durable method

The chapter separates capture, recognition correction, structural revision,
listening, and substantive judgment. It preserves a raw transcript, corrected
transcript, structured draft, listening copy, listening log, revised passage,
and change summary. Multilingual use requires a project-specific benchmark for
names, dates, specialized vocabulary, transliteration, and code-switching.

## Product-dependent guidance

Wispr Flow and Speechify appear in separately maintained tool cards dated
2026-08-01. The cards record the official documentation reviewed for language
support, cloud processing, privacy settings, synchronization, and product limits.
They are scheduled for review on 2026-11-01 or after a material product or policy
change.

## Reader resource

`templates/voice-round-trip-log.md` provides an editable session record, capture
brief, recognition-correction table, listening log, AI-assistance record, change
summary, and final disposition.

## Evidence base

The chapter cites research on voice-based text authoring, LLM-assisted spoken
composition, text-to-speech revision, code-switched speech recognition, and oral
revision by multilingual writers. Bibliographic records were checked against DOI,
publisher, institutional-repository, Google Research, and ACL Anthology pages.
Zotero synchronization was not requested and was not checked.
