# Source Review: Mirrored Newspaper Evidence Case

Reviewed 31 July 2026 for the chapter `content/10-mirrored-newspaper-evidence.qmd`.

## Verification scope

The review used the National Library of Israel historical newspaper collection.
Article metadata, OCR, permanent links, and issue-level PDF links were recorded.
The visible scans were inspected for the Dabburiya reports, the 15 June pipeline
headline, and the 7 July Kawkab al-Hawa report. No newspaper PDF or scan was
copied into the repository.

## Citation decisions

The chapter cites eight Arabic newspaper records and one Hebrew retrospective.
Titles, newspaper names, dates, and permanent links were checked against the
National Library article pages. Where the National Library title was truncated
or affected by OCR, the citation uses a short verified section title or a
descriptive English title and retains the permanent article link.

The existing Beelen et al. citation supports the methodological warning that
digitized newspaper collections reflect selection, scanning, OCR, and interface
bias. It does not support claims about the Mandate-era events.

## Evidentiary limits

The Arabic newspapers are not treated as a unified or unmediated insurgent
voice. The example distinguishes local correspondents, official security
bulletins, and summaries of Hebrew newspapers. The Dana match remains
probable because the Hebrew diary entry is not dated precisely and the body OCR
for the 15 June report is incomplete. Names and ages of civilians reported
injured at Dabburiya remain pending manual transcription.

## Rights

The public package contains original synthesis, metadata, short paraphrases, and
links. It contains no newspaper images or article transcripts. Publication of
scans would require a separate rights review.
