## Scope and verification rule

This plan supplies a bounded reading and citation program for the chapter specification dated 4 August 2026. It does not treat metadata verification as claim verification. Every source below has an authoritative publisher, journal, institutional, or DOI record. Before chapter prose cites a source, the drafter must read the relevant text, record a page-level locator, and confirm that the proposed sentence does not exceed the source's claim.

No source will be added merely to signal membership in a literature. Each source has one named argumentative load. The chapter should rely on approximately eight to twelve sources in its body notes. Additional works may enter only when a draft claim requires them.

## Claim-verification workflow

For each planned chapter claim:

1. Name the exact proposition before searching the source.
2. Read the relevant source section rather than relying on an abstract, review, or search excerpt.
3. Record the source version, page range, and a short paraphrase of what the source supports.
4. Record what the source does not establish.
5. Check the source against Zotero library 2847874 in read-only mode when available. Mark `IN ZOTERO`, `NOT IN ZOTERO`, or `PARTIAL MATCH`.
6. Generate the Chicago 17 note only after the claim and locator pass review.
7. Add the verified source and claim to `editorial/fact-check-log.md`.

## Stop rule

The source plan is sufficient for drafting when the eight Priority 1 works have been read for the assigned argumentative loads, page-level claim notes exist, and at least one methods source has tested each of these risks: disclosure without reflexivity, participation without authority, auditability without ethics, and flexibility without a recorded design decision. Do not add another source unless a named draft claim remains unsupported or a substantive rival position is missing.
