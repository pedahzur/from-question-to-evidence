# Manuscript Audit: *From Question to Evidence*

**Reviewed version:** `1a0a401`

**Review date:** 4 August 2026

**Mode:** Developmental manuscript audit and press-readiness assessment

**Scope:** Complete 27-chapter manuscript, rendered Markdown and PDF, project roadmap, fact-check log, rights manifest, templates, and prior referee report

## Verdict

The manuscript contains a strong and publishable book inside it, but the present working edition is not yet a coherent general methods textbook. Its central contribution is clear: qualitative researchers need a visible, versioned chain connecting question, corpus, transformation, analysis, and claim, especially when AI accelerates discovery and processing. The strongest chapters turn that proposition into concrete artifacts, gates, and exercises.

The principal obstacle is now architectural rather than stylistic. The book promises the full qualitative research lifecycle, but it currently concentrates on evidence mapping, literature review, documentary retrieval, and AI governance. Collection, fieldwork, analysis, disconfirmation, writing from evidence, responsible sharing, and maintenance remain planned rather than delivered. Meanwhile, several specialist chapters have accumulated around the completed core and now compete with it for the book's identity.

**Recommendation: revise, restructure, and complete before external submission.** Do not line-edit the full manuscript yet. First decide the final architecture, move or reclassify the specialist material, and write the missing methodological spine.

## What the Manuscript Does Well

### 1. The central argument is strong

The evidence map is a useful organizing concept. Its value lies less in any single component than in the integration of bounded questions, source families, search records, claim-specific evaluation, coverage audits, stopping rules, and version history. The manuscript repeatedly connects method to a durable object rather than to a transient software interface.

### 2. The artifact-first pedagogy is unusually concrete

The recurring sequence of Orientation, Learn, Worked Example, Try It, Guided AI Workflow, Integrity Checkpoint, Save the Artifact, and Advanced Practice gives readers a reliable entry point. The templates convert abstract advice into usable research records. The insistence that consequential decisions produce reviewable artifacts is the book's strongest pedagogical idea.

### 3. AI is placed under methodological control

The book's model-neutral division of labor is consistent and defensible. AI proposes, stages, compares, and challenges; researchers approve sources, transformations, classifications, and claims. The distinction among candidate, verified, accepted, and published objects is especially valuable. The privacy chapter now correctly states that removing names does not make qualitative data safe to upload.

### 4. The documentary case material is strong

“Mirrored Newspaper Evidence” is the most convincing demonstration of the method in action. It preserves failed searches, source dependence, cross-language matching, and uncertainty without forcing competing records into one narrative. “Historical Sources as Evidence” adds an excellent chain from witness and transcription to observation, interpretation, claim, and downstream audit. The distinction among source-stated, normalized, and inferred content should remain central to the book.

### 5. The prose is disciplined

The prose is clear, restrained, and readable by graduate students and experienced researchers. It avoids promotional claims about AI and usually states limits beside procedures. The problem is not sentence-level quality. It is repetition and unevenness created by the present architecture.

### 6. The project is technically and editorially mature

The manuscript renders in HTML, Markdown, DOCX, and PDF; it has a fact-check log, rights manifest, tests, templates, versioned bundles, and public/private boundaries. This infrastructure is already stronger than that of many finished textbook projects.

## Principal Weaknesses and Gaps

### BLOCKER 1 — The title and description promise a book that has not yet been written

The project description calls this a guide to “building, collecting, analyzing, and communicating qualitative evidence.” The opening says the guide follows a project from question to corpus, analysis, and public claim. The manuscript itself later concedes that it does not collect an interview, prepare a full corpus, or write the final analysis.

The existing book is strongest as a field guide to building and governing an evidence base. It is not yet a general field guide to qualitative research. The missing core includes:

- collection from interviews, observation, archives, images, audio, video, and the web;
- consent, access negotiation, field relations, and researcher-produced records;
- sampling and case selection beyond search coverage;
- preparation, transcription, translation, coding, and memoing across source types;
- thematic, narrative, discourse, comparative, ethnographic, and process-tracing analysis;
- claim testing, negative cases, disconfirmation, and rival explanations as a full module;
- writing from the evidence chain, responsible release, correction, and maintenance.

**Fix:** either complete the full lifecycle promised in the roadmap or narrow the subtitle and description to evidence-base construction. The approved project route favors completion.

### BLOCKER 2 — The whole-book architecture has drifted

Chapters 3–18 form two coherent instructional modules. Chapters 19–25 are a mixture of synthesis chapter, specialist methods chapters, essay, integrity statement, writing workflow, and software companion. They do not yet form a third part of equal conceptual status.

The imbalance is visible in length and function. The foundational stage chapters run roughly 600–1,500 words. “Review Articles and Meta-Analysis in Transition” exceeds 6,000 words and contains at least three chapters: review forms and meta-analysis, skills and agents, and project-context architecture. “From Notes to Research Infrastructure” uses first-person singular and a personal Readwise audit, unlike the book's prevailing coauthored instructional voice. “Skills and Agents Lab” is release documentation rather than a book chapter.

AI integrity also appears as Chapter 22, after eighteen chapters have already assigned AI work.

**Fix:** rebuild the table of contents around parts rather than accumulated modules. Move AI integrity immediately after the epistemological opening. Split the review/meta-analysis chapter. Put installation instructions and the Lab in the companion site. Recast the PKM audit as a short authored interlude or replace its weak source base with a scholarly chapter. Decide whether the voice chapter belongs in the writing module or in the companion methods toolbox.

### BLOCKER 3 — The method still does not travel far enough

The terrorism example dominates the foundational stages. The Arabic–Hebrew newspaper case substantially improves linguistic and archival breadth, but it remains a documentary retrieval problem. The Salem example is hypothetical and explicitly untested. The municipal case appears in fragments rather than as a complete case.

The manuscript therefore has no fully worked case in which the researcher helps produce the evidence through interviews, observation, participation, or oral history. Questions of consent, rapport, refusal, positionality, field notes, unequal access, transcription, member disagreement, and participant risk remain outside the demonstrated method.

**Fix:** carry one composite oral-history or ethnographic project through the entire workflow. Use the same case later in collection, preparation, analysis, claim testing, writing, and sharing. This is the single most important demonstration needed to support the subtitle.

### BLOCKER 4 — The epistemological position remains too thin

The opening acknowledges that interpretive and constructivist traditions may reject parts of the framework, and several advanced sections allow questions to remain open. These concessions are intelligent but insufficient. The book's controlling vocabulary—evidence, coverage, gap, validation, adequacy, and stopping—still implies that a corpus can be assessed against a prior map.

The manuscript needs a sustained account of what the map claims to cover: not the world, but the researcher's declared evidentiary design under historically and socially situated conditions. It also needs to treat the researcher as a record-producing and category-producing actor, not only as the governor of records created elsewhere.

Reflexivity, positionality, feminist and decolonial methods, participatory authority, and community control are nearly absent. Archival silence is handled well; situated inquiry more broadly is not.

**Fix:** add an early chapter on epistemic position, researcher location, power, and the limits of auditability. Then revise every use of “coverage” so the object being covered is explicit.

### BLOCKER 5 — The pedagogy has not been tested with readers

The manuscript calls some modules tested, but the concluding chapter still says reader testing is a future step. Internal tests establish files, links, schemas, and rendering. They do not establish that a novice can complete the exercises or that an experienced researcher finds the advanced track worthwhile.

There are no chapter learning objectives, completion criteria tied to a rubric, instructor notes, answer keys or annotated specimens, sample syllabus, or documented reader pilot. Several exercises assume an active research project, a colleague, lawful full-text access, and comfort with spreadsheets.

**Fix:** run a small structured pilot with novice and experienced readers. Measure completion time, points of confusion, artifact quality, and whether the eight-part scaffold helps or fatigues. Add one annotated model answer, a rubric, and a solo-researcher alternative for each module before expanding instructor apparatus.

## Major Revision Issues

### 1. Citation quality is uneven

The best recent chapters have explicit source reviews and carefully bounded claims. Several core procedural chapters have few or no scholarly citations, even when they make general claims about qualitative design. The PKM chapter relies heavily on commercial posts, social-media threads, a shared AI conversation, and a paper the chapter itself labels unverified. That evidence can document one researcher's information environment, but it cannot carry a general chapter about research infrastructure.

**Fix:** distinguish personal intellectual history from methodological evidence. Build the latter on the qualitative-methods, knowledge-organization, HCI, archival, data-stewardship, and research-infrastructure literature. Complete a chapter-level citation audit rather than relying only on selected claim checks.

### 2. Repetition is beginning to obscure the method

The fixed scaffold creates consistency, but repeated warnings, prompt rules, privacy restrictions, and “record/verify/preserve” instructions now occupy substantial space. The book has seven evidence-map stages, six literature stages, an eight-step newspaper reconstruction, a seven-stage PKM-AI loop, and a coupled-loop historical model. Each is intelligible alone; together they create framework competition.

**Fix:** establish one master research lifecycle and treat the other sequences as domain-specific views. Move repeated AI rules into an early governing protocol and retain only task-specific variations in later chapters. Let the page scaffold flex when a section would otherwise repeat a rule already established.

### 3. Several bridge chapters combine too many methods

The event-database chapter joins retrieval, event resolution, dynamic codebooks, validation, and Bayesian process tracing. The review chapter joins review taxonomy, meta-analysis, open science, living reviews, AI evaluation, skills, agents, and project memory. Both contain strong material, but neither currently teaches one bounded competence.

**Fix:** split by reader task and move advanced statistical or automation material into separate advanced chapters or companion modules.

### 4. The advanced-reader promise is underdeveloped

“Advanced Practice” is usually the shortest section. Experienced researchers receive thoughtful caveats but little sustained engagement with hard cases, competing schools, team disagreement, or research-design tradeoffs.

**Fix:** replace thin advanced codas with fewer, longer advanced dossiers or margin routes keyed to real disputes and failure cases.

### 5. Accessibility is uneven across formats

The web edition includes visible accessibility work, but the current 230-page PDF reports no structural tagging. A living open textbook should not treat accessibility as a web-only feature.

**Fix:** add tagged-PDF and DOCX accessibility checks, verify heading order and table reading order, and publish an accessible-text fallback when the PDF toolchain cannot preserve tags.

### 6. Editorial maintenance signals need cleanup

The source filenames retain duplicate numeric prefixes (`19-*`, `20-*`) that no longer correspond to rendered chapter numbers. One historical table reintroduces “research movement” after the project standardized on “stage.” These do not harm the reader today, but they increase maintenance risk.

**Fix:** renumber source files only after the new architecture is approved, then add a terminology test for stage names and central artifacts.

## Recommended Architecture

### Part I — Judgment, Evidence, and AI

1. Origins and purpose
2. Epistemic position, reflexivity, and limits
3. AI and research integrity
4. The evidence map and master lifecycle

### Part II — Designing the Evidence Base

5. Frame
6. Decompose
7. Map source families
8. Search
9. Evaluate
10. Test coverage and stop
11. Mirrored newspaper case

### Part III — Literature as Evidence

Retain the six-stage literature module, but shorten repeated governance material. Split meta-analysis into an advanced quantitative-synthesis chapter. Move skills, agents, and project-context implementation to the companion Lab.

### Part IV — Collection and Preparation

Add the missing full collection module and the composite oral-history case. Place historical evidence chains and event-database construction here as specialist chapters after the common collection spine.

### Part V — Analysis and Claim Testing

Add close reading, coding, comparison, chronology, process tracing, qualitative counting and visualization, rival explanations, negative cases, and disconfirmation. Make explicit how claims move from local observation to broader inference.

### Part VI — Writing, Sharing, and Maintaining

Place writing by voice inside a broader writing-from-evidence module. Add responsible publication, public data, restricted evidence, correction, versioning, preservation, and maintenance. Use the PKM material selectively to explain continuity across the lifecycle.

### Companion rather than numbered chapters

- Skills and Agents Lab installation and release notes;
- dated tool cards;
- software-specific tutorials;
- full PKM/Readwise audit;
- benchmark packages and validation scripts;
- instructor materials and downloadable specimens.

## Priority Revision Sequence

1. Approve the six-part architecture and classify every current chapter as core, specialist, interlude, companion, or archive.
2. Write the epistemic-position chapter and move AI integrity before the first guided AI workflow.
3. Build Collection as Evidence around one complete composite oral-history case.
4. Pilot the evidence-map and literature modules with novice and experienced readers; revise the scaffold from observed use.
5. Write Analysis and Claim Testing before expanding automation or tool material.

## Press-Readiness Judgment

**Current status: not yet ready for external submission as a general qualitative-methods textbook.** The core concept, prose, and project infrastructure justify continued book development. A press reader would likely respond well to the evidence-map method and the model-neutral integrity controls, then object that the subtitle and description outrun the delivered lifecycle, that the example base remains mostly documentary, and that the late specialist chapters make the book feel assembled rather than designed.

One disciplined developmental revision can solve the architecture. It cannot substitute for writing the missing collection and analysis spine or for testing the pedagogy with readers.
