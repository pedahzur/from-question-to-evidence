# Historical Research Cycle Integration

**Date:** 2026-08-04

**Manuscript target:** *Historical Sources as Evidence*

## Editorial Decision

The new historical-research-cycle material was integrated into the existing bridge chapter rather than added as a separate chapter. The book already presents a seven-stage evidence pathway, and the historical-sources chapter already controls the transformations between archival witness and historical claim. A second full cycle would have duplicated both structures.

The revision adds the concepts that change the chapter's method: asymmetric acceleration between discovery and validation, verification debt, a coupled-loop stage-gate table, the nested source universe, retrieval as a selection process, and three levels of reproducibility.

## Source Use

The retrieval discussion is grounded in Murugaraj et al.'s Topic-RAG evaluation and Zhou, Si, and Hou's humanities-in-the-loop framework. Publication metadata and the claims used in the chapter were checked against the Cambridge University Press and Wiley records on 2026-08-04. Zotero status was not checked because the Zotero connector was unavailable.

## Material Not Imported

The illustrative French Revolution research question and the full Hebrew exposition were not inserted verbatim. They remain useful for a later worked-example module, but the canonical manuscript is English-first and the present chapter already uses the Salem pilot as its worked example. The integration preserves the project's existing page pattern and avoids introducing a competing case before the Salem design is tested.
