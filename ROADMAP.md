# Project Roadmap

*From Question to Evidence* is being developed as an open-access living field guide. Modules are published in reviewed increments; methodological pages change more slowly than templates and dated tool guidance.

## Completed in the Discussion Draft

### Module 1: From Question to Evidence Map

- Frame the question.
- Decompose concepts and contexts.
- Map source families.
- Search iteratively.
- Evaluate evidence.
- Test coverage.
- Produce an evidence map and stopping rule.

### Module 2: Literature as Evidence

- Define the review.
- Map concepts and vocabulary.
- Discover literature through multiple routes.
- Evaluate sources and claims.
- Read, annotate, and compare.
- Synthesize, audit, and stop.

## Next Module

### Collection and Post-Production

This module will connect a collection plan to a documented research corpus. It will address archival work, interviews, observation, images, audio, video, web material, consent, capture, naming, metadata, storage, backup, OCR, transcription, translation, chain of custody, and preservation.

## Later Modules

1. Structuring unstructured material: events, entities, units, fields, uncertainty, and context.
2. Analysis across modes: close reading, coding, comparison, chronology, process tracing, counting, and visualization.
3. Testing claims and seeking disconfirmation.
4. Writing from an audit trail.
5. Sharing without harming.
6. Maintaining a living research object.
7. Hebrew adaptation and right-to-left publication after the English content model stabilizes.

## Release Logic

- `0.1.x`: evidence-map pilot.
- `0.2.x`: literature-review module and public repository organization.
- `0.3.x`: collection and post-production module.
- `1.0.0`: reviewed first edition with the full research lifecycle, stable templates, documented governance, and archived releases.

The roadmap is directional. A module begins only after its design is approved and its acceptance tests are defined.
