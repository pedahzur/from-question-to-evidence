# Task Plan: G1 Book Contract and Terminology Sheet

## Goal

Produce the one-page book contract and normative terminology/capitalization sheet required by WS1, integrate them into the project record, and verify that they implement the approved architecture without changing manuscript prose.

## Phases

- [x] Phase 1: Confirm the approved architecture, audience, scope boundary, lifecycle, and terminology already used in the manuscript.
- [x] Phase 2: Draft the one-page book contract and map its promise to Parts I–VI.
- [x] Phase 3: Draft the terminology and capitalization sheet and resolve competing or ambiguous labels.
- [x] Phase 4: Audit both documents against the architecture plan and current manuscript vocabulary.
- [ ] Phase 5: Verify, commit, publish, and synchronize the project record.

## Key Questions

1. What single sentence states the book's promise without exceeding its approved scope?
2. Which reader competencies must every part deliver?
3. Which labels are canonical, and which legacy labels become domain-specific views or deprecated synonyms?
4. Which terminology distinctions protect the book's epistemic and ethical claims?

## Decisions Made

- The contract will be a working G1 document, not new manuscript prose.
- The primary audience is graduate qualitative-methods students; the secondary audience is experienced researchers adapting their work to AI-supported research.
- The numbered book carries stable method. Volatile products, installation instructions, benchmarks, and dated comparisons remain in companion material.
- The ten-stage research lifecycle is the only book-level sequence. Shorter domain sequences remain named pathways or views, not rival lifecycles.

## Errors Encountered

- None.
