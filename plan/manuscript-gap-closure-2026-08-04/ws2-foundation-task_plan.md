# Task Plan: WS2 Foundation Package

## Goal

Complete the approved specification and verified source plan for “Judgment, Position, and the Limits of Evidence,” create the AI pre-flight gate, and preserve the package without changing manuscript prose.

## Phases

- [x] Phase 1: Inspect the approved WS2 contract, current chapters, bibliography, and source gaps.

## Key Questions

1. What single claim must the chapter establish before the book begins procedural work?
2. Which conceptual conversations are necessary, and which would turn the chapter into a survey?
3. What must the pre-flight gate decide before any guided AI workflow begins?
4. What evidence will show that the package is ready for chapter drafting without prematurely editing manuscript prose?

## Decisions Made

- This task produces a drafting specification, verified source plan, and reusable pre-flight record. It does not draft or relocate manuscript chapters.
- The source plan will assign each work one named argumentative function and a verification status.
- Stable research obligations belong in the pre-flight gate. Product-specific retention settings, prices, interfaces, and model capabilities remain in dated companion material.
- The pre-flight gate is a decision record, not a generic ethics checklist. Any unresolved stop condition prevents external AI processing.
- The chapter specification uses one governing claim and seven argumentative movements rather than a survey of epistemological schools.
- Source metadata is verified, but every manuscript claim still requires full-text reading and a page-level locator.
- G2 remains open until the chapter and revised AI-integrity chapter are drafted, placed before the first guided workflow, and verified.
