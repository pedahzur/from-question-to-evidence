## Binding inputs

- Six parts covering judgment, evidence design, literature, collection/preparation, analysis/claim testing, and writing/sharing/maintenance.
- Ten-stage research lifecycle: Position, Frame, Design evidence, Discover, Collect, Prepare, Analyze, Test claims, Express, Release and maintain.
- Target of approximately 32–36 numbered chapters and 75,000–90,000 body words, excluding references and companion documentation.
- Composite municipal oral-history case with no real participant data.
- Stable methodological core in the book; volatile implementation material in companions.
- Artifact-first pedagogy, bounded AI assistance, human approval at consequential transitions, and source-to-claim traceability.

## Vocabulary issues to resolve

- Existing seven-stage evidence-map, six-stage literature, seven-stage PKM-AI, and historical-source sequences must become pathways or views over the ten-stage lifecycle.
- `source family` means a shared production process, not a file format, repository, or convenient topic group.
- Candidate, verified, accepted, and published objects require distinct states.
- Coverage must refer to the declared evidence design rather than the phenomenon or world.
- `audit`, `stopping rule`, `human gate`, and `release` need operational definitions.
- `AI-assisted` should describe bounded tasks; `AI-augmented` may describe a whole method only when the augmentation is explicitly governed.
- Generic concepts use sentence case. Title case is reserved for book parts, chapter titles, named resources, and exact schema labels.

## Audit findings

- The 664-word contract states one promise and maps it to all six Parts, including principal durable results.
- Its audience, contribution, exclusions, pedagogical requirements, word range, and companion boundary match the approved architecture.
- The promise names all ten lifecycle functions after `discovery` was added explicitly during the audit.
- The terminology sheet makes `research lifecycle` the sole reader-facing book-level sequence and defines domain sequences as crosswalked pathways.
- The record-state vocabulary preserves separate candidate, verified, accepted, and published states.
- Coverage, gaps, and stopping are defined relative to the declared evidence design rather than the world.
- Generic terms use sentence case; formal titles, lifecycle labels, resources, and exact schema fields retain title case.
- No manuscript source file was edited.
