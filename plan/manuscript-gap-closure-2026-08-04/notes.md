## Missing Spine

### Part I: Judgment, evidence, and AI

- New epistemic-position chapter.
- Early AI-integrity chapter.
- One master lifecycle with domain-specific views.

### Part IV: Collection and preparation

- Permission, consent, access, and source/participant relations.
- Sampling and case selection appropriate to different qualitative designs.
- Capture of interviews, observation, documents, images, audio, video, web, and born-digital material.
- Field notes, interview records, researcher memos, refusal, withdrawal, and unequal access.
- OCR, transcription, translation, normalization, coding preparation, and chain of custody.
- Storage, access control, backup, preservation, deletion obligations, and corpus handoff.

### Part V: Analysis and claim testing

- Analytic questions, units, and claim types.
- Close reading, coding, thematic development, and memoing.
- Narrative, discourse, comparative, and chronological analysis.
- Case comparison, process tracing, qualitative counting, and visualization.
- Rival explanations, negative cases, disconfirmation, and uncertainty.
- Claim assembly and downstream audit.

### Part VI: Writing, sharing, and maintenance

- Writing from evidence and claim registers.
- Integrating quotations, tables, figures, and uncertainty.
- Responsible sharing of public, restricted, and participant-derived material.
- Corrections, versions, citation, preservation, and retirement.
- Long-term project context and research memory.

## Sequence Logic

1. Architecture precedes drafting because every new chapter depends on its final function.
2. Epistemic position and AI integrity precede all guided workflows.
3. The oral-history case precedes the collection module because it supplies the module's running evidence.
4. Collection and preparation precede analysis because the analysis module requires a documented corpus.
5. Analysis precedes writing and public release because writing must demonstrate the source-to-claim chain.
6. The pilot follows a complete representative pathway but precedes final line editing.
7. Citation, accessibility, and release audits occur after substantive structure stabilizes.
