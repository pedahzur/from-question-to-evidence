## Initial scope

The crosswalk must cover the evidence-map pathway, literature pathway, mirrored-newspaper case, review and synthesis material, event-database pathway, historical-sources pathway, PKM-AI pathway, writing-by-voice pathway, and planned composite oral-history route. It must also identify where AI integrity governs every stage without becoming a competing sequence.

## Inventory conclusions

- The evidence-map pathway covers framing, evidence design, discovery, coverage testing, and the collection handoff. It does not collect or analyze a corpus.
- The literature pathway spans review definition through synthesis and, in its advanced form, living-review maintenance.
- The mirrored-newspaper pathway is a bounded documentary reconstruction route. It demonstrates multilingual discovery, record verification, source dependence, and stopping.
- The event-database pathway spans unit definition, source framing, candidate retrieval, verification, event resolution, codebook versioning, validation, analysis, and release maintenance.
- The historical-sources pathway uses nested discovery and validation loops while preserving the source witness, diplomatic transcription, passage, observation, interpretation, claim, and audit as linked but distinct objects.
- The PKM-AI operations, Bound, Capture, Distill, Connect, Compute, Verify, and Express, recur as infrastructure across the research lifecycle.
- The writing-by-voice pathway is a specialist composition and revision route centered on capture, correction, structure, listening, judgment, versioning, and retention.
- The composite municipal oral-history case is the only planned pathway designed to traverse all ten stages as the book's full transfer test.
- AI integrity supplies authorization, privacy, rights, provenance, validation, disclosure, and maintenance controls at each stage. It does not define the intellectual sequence of the research.

## Relocation conclusions

- Evidence mapping and the mirrored-newspaper interlude remain in Part II.
- Literature and durable review synthesis remain in Part III.
- Historical sources and event-database construction belong mainly in Part IV, with analytical and claim-testing components in Part V.
- Bayesian updating belongs in Part V after the qualitative logic of causal claims and rivals.
- Writing by voice and durable project-memory method belong in Part VI.
- Skills, agents, product guidance, installation, extended quantitative implementation, the full Readwise audit, and release automation belong in companion material.
