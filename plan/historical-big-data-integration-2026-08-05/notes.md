# Integration Notes

## Editorial synthesis

- The 2020 article supplies the structured/unstructured big-data distinction,
  the theory-and-context argument, and the role of historical process evidence.
- The 2022 working paper supplies the pre-analytical paradox of plenty and the
  networked research-memory design.
- The Historical Evidence Chain supplies the operational separation of source,
  transcription, observation, normalization, inference, validation, claim, and
  downstream audit.

## Architecture decision

Keep the approved six-Part architecture and ten-stage lifecycle. Treat historical
research, event data, oral history, and literature review as pathways through the
same stages. Keep stable method in the book and volatile prompts, schemas,
platform implementations, and benchmarks in versioned companions.

until a separate license is recorded.
